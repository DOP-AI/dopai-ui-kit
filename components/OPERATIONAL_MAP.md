# Operational Map Component

## Mission

The Operational Map component provides geospatial operational awareness across DOP-AI dashboards, telemetry systems, escalation platforms, environmental intelligence interfaces, and public visibility systems.

Built under the foundational doctrine:

> Dignity Over Power

The component prioritizes:

- geospatial clarity
- operational awareness
- escalation visibility
- infrastructure understanding
- environmental transparency
- public readability
