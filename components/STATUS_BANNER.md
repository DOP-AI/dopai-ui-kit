# Status Banner Component

## Mission

The Status Banner component provides immediate operational awareness across DOP-AI dashboards, telemetry systems, public interfaces, and operational command views.

Built under the foundational doctrine:

> Dignity Over Power

The component prioritizes:

- escalation clarity
- rapid recognition
- operational calm
- public readability
- fail-soft visibility

---

# Core Principle

```text id="x2m6vr"
Critical operational status should remain immediately understandable.
```

---

# Component Purpose

The Status Banner acts as:

- the primary escalation indicator
- operational state anchor
- dashboard attention hierarchy layer
- public operational awareness signal

It should remain:

- visible
- readable
- calm
- consistent

# Primary Fields

## Status Level

Potential values:

- Green
- Yellow
- Orange
- Red

Purpose:
Provide immediate escalation visibility.

Visual priority:
Highest.

---

## Status Summary

Purpose:
Provide human-readable operational explanation.

Example:

```text
Elevated operational stress detected.
```

The language should remain:
- plain
- calm
- readable
- non-alarmist

---

## Alert Count

Purpose:
Provide awareness of active operational conditions.

Example:

```text
Active Alerts: 3
```

---

## Telemetry Health

Purpose:
Provide visibility into telemetry reliability.

Potential values:

- Stable
- Degraded
- Partial Visibility
- Offline

---

# Escalation Visual Hierarchy

## Green

Visual tone:
- calm
- stable
- low aggression

---

## Yellow

Visual tone:
- cautionary
- visible
- controlled

---

## Orange

Visual tone:
- operational urgency
- strong visibility
- serious but calm

---

## Red

Visual tone:
- critical awareness
- restrained urgency
- highly readable

Avoid:
- flashing visuals
- panic aesthetics
- excessive animation

# Responsive Behavior

## Desktop

Banner spans full operational width.

---

## Tablet

Reduced padding with preserved readability.

---

## Mobile

Stacked layout:

```text
STATUS: YELLOW

Telemetry: Stable
```

Operational visibility should remain clear on smaller screens.

---

# Accessibility Principles

The component should support:

- colorblind accessibility
- screen-reader compatibility
- multilingual readability
- low cognitive load

Operational meaning should NEVER depend solely on color.

---

# Motion Philosophy

Optional subtle transitions may support:

- escalation transitions
- telemetry updates
- operational refresh states

Motion should NEVER:
- distract
- create panic perception
- reduce readability
