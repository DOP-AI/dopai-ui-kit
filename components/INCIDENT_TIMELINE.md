# Incident Timeline Component

## Mission

The Incident Timeline component provides chronological operational visibility across DOP-AI dashboards, public interfaces, escalation systems, and resilience coordination platforms.

Built under the foundational doctrine:

> Dignity Over Power

The component prioritizes:

- operational clarity
- escalation visibility
- chronological awareness
- transparency
- public readability

---

Purpose:
Provide operational chronology.

```text
14:32 UTC
```

- readable
- standardized
- operationally consistent
