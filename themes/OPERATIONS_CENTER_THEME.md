# Operations Center Theme

## Mission

The Operations Center Theme defines the primary visual environment for DOP-AI operational dashboards, telemetry systems, escalation interfaces, and resilience coordination platforms.

Built under the foundational doctrine:

> Dignity Over Power

The theme prioritizes:

- operational clarity
- calm hierarchy
- escalation visibility
- geospatial readability
- low cognitive load
- institutional consistency

---

# Core Principle

```text id="f6n2qx"
Operational interfaces should remain calm during instability.
```

---

# Theme Philosophy

Traditional operations interfaces often fail because they become:

- visually chaotic
- overly militarized
- cognitively exhausting
- cluttered with telemetry
- difficult to interpret under stress

DOP-AI themes prioritize:

- restrained hierarchy
- dark operational calm
- readable telemetry
- layered visibility
- institutional clarity

The interface should feel:

```text id="g9p4vr"
stable during instability
```
