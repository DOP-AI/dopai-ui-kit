# Status System

## Mission

The DOP-AI status system exists to provide human-readable operational awareness across dashboards, telemetry systems, escalation engines, public interfaces, and geospatial systems.

Built under the foundational doctrine:

> Dignity Over Power

The status hierarchy prioritizes:

- clarity
- operational calm
- escalation visibility
- rapid recognition
- public readability
- coordinated response

---

# Core Principle

```text
Status systems should improve understanding without increasing panic.
```

---

# Status Philosophy

Traditional operational systems often fail because escalation systems become:

- visually aggressive
- cognitively overwhelming
- inconsistent
- difficult to interpret
- overly technical

DOP-AI systems prioritize:

- calm visibility
- operational clarity
- layered escalation
- restrained urgency
- human-readable language

The interface should feel:

```text
stable during instability
```

---

# Primary Escalation Hierarchy

## Green — Stable

### Meaning

Normal operational conditions.

### Characteristics

- telemetry stable
- infrastructure operational
- manageable environmental conditions
- no active escalation

### Visual Tone

- calm
- low aggression
- readable

### Recommended Color Direction

Deep operational green.

---

## Yellow — Elevated

### Meaning

Increased operational stress or early instability indicators.

### Characteristics

- rising environmental stress
- increased telemetry anomalies
- elevated system load
- early warning conditions

### Visual Tone

- noticeable
- cautionary
- controlled

### Recommended Color Direction

Amber / muted gold.

---

## Orange — Significant Instability

### Meaning

Substantial operational concern requiring coordinated awareness.

### Characteristics

- correlated anomalies
- infrastructure strain
- elevated environmental risk
- escalation preparation

### Visual Tone

- urgent
- highly visible
- operationally serious

### Recommended Color Direction

Signal orange.

---

## Red — Critical

### Meaning

Critical operational instability requiring emergency coordination.

### Characteristics

- severe infrastructure stress
- major escalation conditions
- emergency operational state
- containment prioritization

### Visual Tone

- critical visibility
- calm urgency
- non-chaotic

### Recommended Color Direction

Controlled dark red.

---

# Dashboard Status Rules

Escalation indicators should remain:

- large
- readable
- color-consistent
- operationally calm

Avoid:
- flashing alerts
- panic aesthetics
- excessive animation
- alarmist visual language

---

# Public Interface Principles

Public escalation systems should prioritize:

- plain-language terminology
- clear hierarchy
- operational calm
- trust-building communication

Example:

```text
REGIONAL STATUS: YELLOW
Elevated operational stress detected.
```

Avoid:
- militarized terminology
- fear-based messaging
- unnecessary technical jargon

---

# Geospatial Escalation

Maps should support:

- escalation overlays
- regional status zones
- operational boundaries
- infrastructure stress visibility

Escalation visibility should improve:
- situational awareness
- coordination
- comprehension

---

# Telemetry Integration

Status systems may integrate with:

- telemetry confidence scoring
- anomaly detection
- escalation forecasting
- operational coordination systems

Status should remain explainable and reviewable.

---

# Motion Philosophy

Status transitions may use subtle motion for:

- escalation changes
- telemetry updates
- operational state transitions

Motion should NEVER:
- distract
- overwhelm
- increase panic perception

---

# Accessibility Principles

Status systems should support:

- colorblind accessibility
- multilingual readability
- public usability
- low cognitive load

Operational meaning should NEVER depend solely on color.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The DOP-AI status system is intended to support:

- operational dashboards
- telemetry systems
- geospatial interfaces
- public transparency platforms
- resilience coordination systems
- public operational visibility

Operational escalation systems should improve coordinated response faster than instability spreads.
